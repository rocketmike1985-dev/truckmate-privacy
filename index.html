TruckMate UK Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

TruckMate UK ("TruckMate", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the TruckMate UK mobile application.

Important: TruckMate UK is designed for vehicle and fleet management, driver compliance, vehicle checks, jobs and loads, and HGV navigation.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, TruckMate UK may collect and process the following types of information.

Account Information

When you create or use a TruckMate account, we may process information associated with your account, such as your email address and authentication information.

If you use Google Sign-In, authentication information is processed through Google's authentication services.

Fleet and Vehicle Information

TruckMate may store information that you enter about vehicles and your fleet, including vehicle details, registration information, MOT and service information, mileage, vehicle status, checks, and related maintenance information.

Jobs and Loads

Information entered into the Jobs & Loads features may include job numbers, customer information, collection and delivery addresses, collection and delivery dates and times, assigned vehicles, assigned drivers, load descriptions, weight information, notes, and job status.

Driver and Compliance Information

Information entered into TruckMate about drivers may include driver names, driver-management information, compliance information, driver hours, and other information that you choose to enter for fleet-management purposes.

Defect Reports and Vehicle Checks

TruckMate may store information that you enter when carrying out vehicle checks or reporting vehicle defects, including descriptions, notes, photographs or other information submitted through the relevant features.

Location Information

When you use the HGV Sat Nav features, TruckMate may use your device's location information to provide navigation, route guidance, live positioning, and automatic rerouting.

Location information is used to provide navigation functionality and is not used for advertising purposes.

Subscription and Purchase Information

TruckMate offers optional Premium subscriptions. Purchases are processed through Google Play on supported Android devices.

We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Payment processing is handled by the relevant payment platform.

2. How We Use Information

We use information collected through TruckMate to:

  • Provide and operate the TruckMate UK application.
  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Store and display fleet and vehicle information.
  • Provide vehicle checks and defect-reporting functionality.
  • Manage jobs, loads, drivers, and compliance information.
  • Provide HGV navigation and route guidance.
  • Provide live location-based navigation and rerouting when enabled.
  • Provide Premium subscription functionality.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the application.
  • Respond to support requests and service-related enquiries.

3. Third-Party Services

TruckMate uses third-party services to provide parts of the application. These services may process information on our behalf or as part of providing their own services.

Google Firebase

TruckMate uses Google Firebase services for functionality including authentication and cloud data storage.

Information stored using Firebase may include account information and fleet, vehicle, job, driver, compliance, check, and defect information entered into the application.

Google Play

Premium subscriptions purchased through Android are processed through Google Play. Google may process purchase and payment information in accordance with its own privacy policies and terms.

Navigation and Mapping Services

TruckMate's HGV Sat Nav uses mapping and routing services to calculate routes and provide navigation functionality. Information required to calculate and update routes may include your current location, destination, and selected vehicle characteristics.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate TruckMate, including authentication, cloud-storage, navigation, mapping, and subscription services.

We may also disclose information where required to do so by law or where reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or property of TruckMate, its users, or others.

5. Data Storage and Security

TruckMate uses appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the TruckMate service, maintain user accounts, provide requested features, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

Information that is no longer required may be deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

7. Your Choices, Rights, and Account Deletion

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, or a copy of information we hold about you.

Requesting Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your TruckMate UK account and associated personal data by contacting us at:

Email: quicknorva@hotmail.com

When requesting account deletion, please use the email address associated with your TruckMate account where possible and state that you want your TruckMate account and associated data deleted.

We will process account deletion requests in accordance with applicable law. Some information may need to be retained where required by law, for legitimate legal purposes, or where necessary to resolve disputes or enforce applicable agreements.

Deleting your account may result in the loss of access to your TruckMate data and account-based features, including fleet, vehicle, job, driver, compliance, defect-report, and other information associated with your account.

You may also stop using TruckMate at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

TruckMate UK is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of providing a child-directed service.

9. Advertising

TruckMate UK does not currently display third-party advertising and does not use personal information or location information for targeted advertising.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to TruckMate, applicable law, or the services we use.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
